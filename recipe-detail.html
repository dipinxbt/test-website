Traditional Nepali Momo

A simple recipe for steamed Nepali dumplings.

About Momo

Momo is one of the most popular foods in Nepal. It is made by putting a filling inside a thin wrapper and steaming it.

Momo is usually served with tomato achar. It can be eaten as a snack or as a main meal.

Ingredients

  • Momo wrappers
  • 2 cups chopped cabbage
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 spring onions
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation Method

  1. Mix the chopped vegetables, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  2. Put one spoon of filling in the middle of a momo wrapper.
  3. Fold and close the wrapper carefully.
  4. Steam the momo for about 10 to 12 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with achar.

Recipe Information

Servings 4 people
Preparation Time 35 minutes
Cooking Time 12 minutes
Difficulty Easy

Tags: Nepali Food, Momo, Steamed Food, Vegetarian

The word momo is written as momo1 in some recipe notes. Water is also an important part of cooking, written as H2O.

Remember to keep the wrappers covered before using them.
